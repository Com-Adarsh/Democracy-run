THE QUICK START

Keep moving.

Run automatically through Delhi. Time your moves, collect flags, and beat your personal best.

  1. JumpPress Space, ↑, or W. On a phone, tap JUMP or the game area. Clear cones, barricades, and police obstacles.
  2. SlidePress ↓ or S, or tap SLIDE. Crouch under hanging banners marked ↓. Banners appear after 240 points.
  3. Collect & scoreEarn 24 points each second and 10 per flag. Chain flags within 4 seconds for streak bonuses up to 20 points. Near misses add 15 points. The street gets faster as your score grows.
  4. PausePress P or Esc, or use Ⅱ. Switching away from the game pauses your run automatically.
